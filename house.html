Description

Casa Coral is a bright two floor house just ten minutes away from the beach. The ground floor has an open living and dining area, a modern kitchen and a small garden with space for a grill.

Upstairs you will find three bedrooms, two of them with their own balcony, and two full bathrooms. The house includes a covered garage for two cars, air conditioning in every room and solar water heating.
